## Stockfish

Stockfish is **not** included in this repository and is not a dependency.

The development scripts under `tools/` invoke a Stockfish binary that you supply
yourself, purely as an external sparring partner and as a labelling oracle when
generating NNUE training data. Stockfish is never linked into the engine, never
called at runtime, and never redistributed here.

## Syzygy tablebases

The `.rtbw` / `.rtbz` files are not included (the 3-4-5 set alone is ~940 MB).
They are generated by Ronald de Man and distributed freely; fetch them from
[syzygy-tables.info](https://syzygy-tables.info/) or a mirror.
